Delete Your Ardy Account

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Ardy account and the data associated with it.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to ardyy2026@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Ardy account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

Please include:

  • The email address registered to your Ardy account
  • The phone number on your account (so we can verify the request)

We verify that the request comes from the account holder before deleting anything. If you email us from a different address than the one on your account, we will ask you to confirm from the registered address.

What Happens Next

  • We confirm receipt of your request within 7 days.
  • Your account and associated data are permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted it cannot be restored, and any listings you posted will no longer be available.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account details — name, email address, phone number, role, and password hash
  • Your listings — titles, descriptions, prices, locations, and any photos you uploaded (including copies stored with our image provider, Cloudinary)
  • Your Land Advisor conversations — all chat messages you sent, the assistant's responses, and the stated preferences saved from those conversations
  • Your favorites — the record of which listings you saved
  • Your buy requests — any requests you submitted on other users' listings

What We May Retain

We may retain a limited amount of information after deletion where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our terms. Any such data is kept only for as long as that purpose requires, and is then deleted.

Records that are no longer linked to you — for example aggregate counts, or a listing's history once your identifying details have been removed — may be retained in de-identified form.

Questions

If you have questions about deleting your account or about how we handle your data, contact us at ardyy2026@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
